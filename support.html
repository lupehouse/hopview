Getting started

  1. Download Hopview for Mac and open it. Allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when macOS asks (System Settings › Privacy & Security).
  2. Choose a PIN (at least 6 characters) and sign in to Tailscale when your browser opens.
  3. Open Hopview on your iPhone or iPad, sign in to the same Tailscale account, then enter the PIN once.

Common questions

“No Mac running Hopview was found”

Make sure Hopview is running on your Mac (look for its icon in the menu bar) and that both devices are signed in to the same Tailscale account.

The screen is black or doesn't update

On the Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording and make sure Hopview is turned on. Then quit and reopen Hopview.

Clicks and typing don't reach the Mac

Turn on Hopview under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

Does my screen go through your servers?

No. We don't run any servers. Your devices connect directly when possible; otherwise Tailscale relays the end-to-end encrypted traffic without being able to read it.

Subscription

Hopview Pro includes a free trial for new subscribers. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore on a new device, tap “Restore Purchases” on the subscription screen. Family Sharing is supported.
