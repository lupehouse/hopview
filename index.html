Hopview

Your Mac, near-instant, from iPhone and iPad.

Hopview streams your Mac's screen straight to your iPhone or iPad over your own private Tailscale network. Up to 60 fps and 4K, end-to-end encrypted. No Hopview account and no Hopview server in between.

1. Install Hopview on your Mac (free)

macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Download for Mac

2. Get Hopview for iPhone and iPad

App Store

3. Sign in to the same Tailscale account on both

Hopview asks you to sign in to Tailscale (free for personal use) the first time. Set a PIN on your Mac, enter it once on each device, and you're connected.

Fast by design

Your devices connect directly to each other whenever the network allows, often on the same Wi‑Fi or across the internet without any detour. Fewer hops means less delay.

Private by design

The connection is end-to-end encrypted with WireGuard. If a direct path isn't possible, encrypted traffic is relayed by Tailscale, which can't read it. Hopview has no servers of its own and collects no data.
